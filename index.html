How It Works

Connect in under
60 seconds.

Follow these simple steps and your phone will be on your screen in no time.

1
Enable Developer Options
Go to Settings → About Phone and tap "Build number" 7 times to unlock Developer Options on your Android phone.
2
Turn On Wireless Debugging
Go to Settings → Developer Options → Wireless Debugging and switch it on. Make sure your phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi.
3
Pair with Pairing Code
Tap "Pair device with pairing code" on your phone, then enter the IP, Pair Port, and 6-digit code into WirelessConnect.
4
Open Live Screen & Control
Hit "Pair & Connect" — your device appears in the sidebar. Click "Open Live Screen" and start mirroring!

Troubleshooting

Having trouble?
We've got you.

Here are the most common issues and exactly how to fix them.

🔐 "Pairing failed. Check the 6-digit code." error

The pairing code expires within 60–90 seconds. If you were slow entering the details, the code expired.

✅ Fix:
  1. On your phone, tap "Pair device with pairing code" again to get a fresh code.
  2. Quickly fill in the new IP, Pair Port, Pairing Code, and Debug Port in the app.
  3. Hit "Pair & Connect" immediately.
📱 "error: protocol fault (couldn't read status message)"

This is a generic ADB error that happens when the app tries to connect to your phone's IP and Port, but the connection fails or the phone doesn't respond.

✅ Fixes:
  1. Wrong Port (Most Common): When you tap "Pair device with pairing code", the popup shows a different port than the main screen! Use the popup port for pairing.
  2. Not on the same Wi-Fi: Ensure both devices are on the exact same Wi-Fi. Some routers have "AP Isolation" enabled. Try a mobile hotspot to test.
  3. macOS Firewall: Try disabling your firewall temporarily. On macOS 15 (Sequoia), check Local Network permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  4. Check Reachability: Open Terminal and run ping [YOUR_PHONE_IP]. "Request timeout" means your Mac can't see the phone at all.
💻 PC on Ethernet, Phone on Wi-Fi (Different Networks)

If your PC is on Ethernet and phone is on Wi-Fi, they can communicate only if on the same router/subnet. Here are the best workarounds:

✅ Solutions:
  1. Virtual LAN (Recommended): Install Tailscale or ZeroTier on both devices. They assign a virtual IP so you can connect from anywhere.
  2. PC Mobile Hotspot: If your PC has Wi-Fi, enable "Mobile Hotspot" and connect your phone directly to it for a direct 0ms connection.
  3. USB Wi-Fi Adapter: Buy a cheap USB Wi-Fi dongle (~$5), enable Mobile Hotspot from the PC, and connect your phone.
  4. USB Tethering (Temporary): Plug your phone into the PC via USB and enable "USB Tethering" in network settings to create a direct local network.
